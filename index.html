Features

Built for the whole workout.

Lift in the gym, run outside, walk the neighborhood, or plan a rest day. MyFit keeps it all in one place.

Strength training

Build reusable routines, log every set, and track warm-up, drop, failure, and AMRAP sets. Supersets, rest timers, and a plate calculator are built in.

Cardio

Track outdoor runs and walks with GPS distance, pace, heart rate, calories, and route maps. Log indoor sessions without GPS.

Progress

See personal records, training volume, streaks, weekly charts, and a muscle heat map that show the work adding up.

Planning

Schedule routines, cardio, rest days, and custom workouts. Add workout times and get reminders before training.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is MyFit free?

Yes. MyFit is free to use with ads. A one-time in-app purchase removes all ads.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Yes. Start strength, indoor, and outdoor workouts from your wrist. Live workout data stays in sync with your iPhone, and you can finish on either device.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. MyFit opens straight into your training, and your workout data is stored on your device.

Can I track outdoor runs and walks?

Yes. Outdoor activities use GPS for distance and route maps, and show pace, heart rate, and calories. Indoor runs and walks can be logged without GPS too.

What does Apple Health integration do?

MyFit can save completed workouts, heart rate, calories, and routes to Apple Health, and read heart rate and calorie data for workout summaries. You control the permissions.

When can I download it?

MyFit is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch. Have a question before launch? Get in touch.
