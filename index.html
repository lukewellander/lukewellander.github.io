Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is MyFit free?

Yes. MyFit is free to use with ads. If you'd rather not see them, a one-time in-app purchase removes all ads.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Yes. You can start strength, indoor, and outdoor workouts from your wrist. Live workout data stays in sync with your iPhone, and you can finish on either device.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. MyFit opens straight into your training, and your workout data is stored on your device.

Can I track outdoor runs and walks?

Yes. Outdoor activities use GPS for distance and route maps, and show pace, heart rate, and calories. Indoor runs and walks can be logged without GPS too.

What does Apple Health integration do?

MyFit can save completed workouts, heart rate, calories, and routes to Apple Health, and read heart rate and calorie data for workout summaries. You control the permissions.

When can I download it?

MyFit is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch. Have a question before launch? Get in touch.
