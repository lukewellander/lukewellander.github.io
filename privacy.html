Privacy Policy

MyFit is built to keep your training data on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses, what stays local, and the limited data involved in advertising.

Effective date: November 10, 2026

MyFit is a workout tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch developed by Luke Wellander. This Privacy Policy describes how the MyFit app and this website handle information.

Information stored on your device

MyFit does not require an account. Workout history, routines, exercises, personal records, body weight entries, schedules, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to a MyFit server.

Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange workout data directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. That data remains within your devices and the app.

Apple Health

With your permission, MyFit can save completed workouts, heart rate, calories, and workout routes to Apple Health. MyFit may also read heart rate and calorie information to display workout summaries.

Apple Health data is used only to provide these workout features. It is not used for advertising, marketing, or analytics, and it is not shared with third parties. You can change or revoke Health permissions at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Location

Outdoor run and walk features request access to your location while the workout is active. Location is used to measure distance and draw your route. Route data is stored with the workout on your device and, if you allow it, saved to Apple Health. MyFit does not operate a location-tracking server.

Advertising

The free version of MyFit displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. This information is handled under Google's privacy policy.

You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy. Where required, the app will ask for permission before using data for personalized advertising. You can remove ads entirely with a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. MyFit does not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and accounts

MyFit does not include a third-party analytics SDK and does not create user accounts. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your workout data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual workouts in the app, or delete all MyFit data from Settings > Data > Delete Data. Deleting the app also removes its local data. Data that was previously saved to Apple Health is managed by Apple Health and can be deleted there.

Children

MyFit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses in server logs as part of providing the service. The site does not use cookies or third-party analytics.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on this site before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email getmyfitapp@gmail.com.

MyFit is currently in development. This policy will be reviewed before the app is released on the App Store.
