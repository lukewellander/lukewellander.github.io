The game

Simple to start. Hard to put down.

Every run begins with one ball. Flick it upward, keep it above the bottom edge, and stay calm when friends join in.

1Ball to start
2At score 5

1Drop ends the run

Flick up

Swipe upward anywhere on the screen. The ball always launches upward, and your swipe adds a little sideways drift.

Keep it up

Time each flick before the ball falls past the bottom. Every clean flick adds a point to your score.

Beat your best

Your high score is saved on your device. Set a new personal best and post it to the public ranks.

One rule to remember: the first dropped ball ends the run, even if the others are still in the air. Every extra ball has to stay up too.

Features

Small game. Sharp edges.

One-thumb flicks

No buttons, no thumbstick, no tutorial wall. Touch, swipe, and the ball goes up.

Extra balls

A second ball joins at 5 points. A third arrives at 15. The screen gets busier and the flicks get tighter.

Public ranks

Choose a nickname and send your best score to the global top 10. No account and no sign-in.

Pixel art

Chunky red balls, a white playfield, and a no-nonsense retro arcade feel.

Haptic feedback

A small tap when you score and a heavier one when the run ends. The phone feels the game with you.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no profile. Your high score and nickname live on your device.

Leaderboard

There is a scoreboard.

Juggler keeps a public top 10. Pick a nickname, set a personal best, and the score is submitted automatically. No account, no email, no profile.

Names and scores are public. Use a nickname you are comfortable sharing, not your real name.
