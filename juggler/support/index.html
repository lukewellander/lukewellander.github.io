Delete your data

Juggler stores your high score and nickname on your device. To remove that local data, delete the app from your iPhone. There is no account to delete.

Leaderboard entries are stored by dreamlo, a third-party service. Deleting the app does not remove them. To ask for a leaderboard entry to be removed, email us with the nickname and approximate score.

Requirements

Juggler is designed for iPhone. The App Store listing shows the minimum iOS version for the build you download. The game works offline, but the public leaderboard needs an internet connection.

Ads

Juggler is free and supported by Google AdMob. A banner appears on the menu and game-over screen, and an interstitial can appear between rounds. Ads are hidden during gameplay.

You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Commercial billing questions are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

How do I play?

Flick the ball upward with a swipe. Every clean flick scores a point. Keep the ball above the bottom edge of the screen for as long as you can.

Why did my run end when another ball was still up?

The first dropped ball ends the run, even if other balls are still in the air. That is the original rule, and it is what makes the extra balls at 5 and 15 points matter.

When do extra balls appear?

A second ball joins at 5 points. A third ball joins at 15 points. Every ball has to stay up, so the run ends as soon as any one of them drops.

How do I change my leaderboard name?

Open RANKS, edit the name field, and tap SAVE. Your next personal best will be posted under that name. Only a new personal best is submitted automatically.

My score did not appear on the leaderboard.

Only a new personal best is uploaded. If the score was not better than your saved high score, it will not be sent. Check your internet connection, then tap REFRESH on the ranks screen.

The leaderboard is blank or says no connection.

The ranks screen uses the dreamlo leaderboard service. If dreamlo is slow or unavailable, the game still works but the top 10 may not load. Wait a moment and try REFRESH again.

How do I delete my data?

Delete Juggler from your iPhone to remove the high score and nickname stored on the device. Leaderboard entries are kept by dreamlo and need a removal request by email.

Why do I see ads?

Juggler is free and supported by advertising through Google AdMob. The game keeps ads off the playfield: banners appear on the menu and game-over screen, and interstitials can appear between rounds.

Does Juggler work offline?

Yes, the game itself works without a connection. The public leaderboard needs internet access, so the ranks screen may be empty while you are offline.

How do I report a bug?

Email getmyfitapp@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot helps if the issue is visual.
