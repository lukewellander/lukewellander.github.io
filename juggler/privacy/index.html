Privacy Policy

Juggler is a small arcade game, and it keeps things simple. This page explains what stays on your device, what the public leaderboard shares, and how advertising works.

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Juggler is an iPhone game developed by Luke Wellander. This Privacy Policy describes how the Juggler app and this website handle information.

The short version: Juggler does not require an account. Your high score and nickname stay on your device unless you post a score to the public leaderboard. The free game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob.

Information stored on your device

Juggler stores your high score, your chosen leaderboard nickname, and a small amount of app preference data on your device. This information is kept in the app's local storage and is not sent to a Juggler server. There is no Juggler account system.

Deleting Juggler from your device removes this local data. Deleting the app does not remove a score that was already posted to the public leaderboard.

Public leaderboard

Juggler uses dreamlo.com, a third-party leaderboard service, to display a public top 10. When you set a new personal best, the app sends your nickname and score to dreamlo. When you open the ranks screen, the app requests the current top 10 from dreamlo.

Leaderboard names and scores are public. They can be seen by anyone who opens the ranks screen. Use a nickname you are comfortable sharing, and do not use your real name, email address, phone number, or other personal information as your nickname. Juggler does not send your email address or contact details to the leaderboard.

As with any web request, dreamlo also receives technical information such as your IP address and a user-agent string when the app contacts the service.

The current build sends leaderboard requests over HTTP rather than HTTPS. That means the nickname and score travel without transport encryption. This is a limitation of the existing dreamlo leaderboard configuration, and it is another reason to keep your nickname free of personal information.

dreamlo's handling of leaderboard data is governed by dreamlo's own practices. You can learn more at dreamlo.com.

Advertising

The free version of Juggler displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, and diagnostics in order to deliver ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud.

Juggler may ask for permission to track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you do not allow tracking, ads may still appear, but they should not use your advertising identifier for personalized advertising. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's handling of advertising data is described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Juggler does not include a third-party analytics SDK.

Your choices

You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can remove local game data by deleting the app. You can ask us to remove a leaderboard entry by email. We do not sell personal information. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access or delete your information; contact us to make a request.

Purchases

Juggler is free to play and is supported by advertising. The current version does not offer in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated before purchase data is handled differently.

Analytics and accounts

Juggler does not create user accounts, does not ask for your email address, and does not include a third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your high score and nickname stay on your device until you delete them or remove the app. To remove local data, delete Juggler from your iPhone. There is no separate account to delete.

Leaderboard entries are kept by dreamlo and may remain visible after you delete the app. To ask for a leaderboard entry to be removed, email getmyfitapp@gmail.com with the nickname and approximate score. We will remove the entry if we can identify it.

Children

Juggler is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because leaderboard names are public, players should not use personal information as a nickname. If you believe a child has posted personal information to the leaderboard, contact us and we will help remove it.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses in server logs as part of providing the service. This site does not use cookies or third-party analytics.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
